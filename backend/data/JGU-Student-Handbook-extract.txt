=== ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY ===
O.P. Jindal Global (Institution of Eminence Deemed to be University) (JGU) is a non-profit
multidisciplinary and research-oriented global university established in 2009 by the Government
of Haryana and entitled by the University Grants Commission (UGC). JGU is a philanthropic
initiative of its Founding Chancellor and Benefactor, Mr. Naveen Jindal, established in memory of
his father, Mr. O. P. Jindal. JGU is a truly international university with over 13,000+ students and
1200+ full-time faculty from 50+ countries in the world and from all states and union territories of India.

JGU has established twelve interdisciplinary schools and four research & capacity building
institutes: Jindal Global Law School (JGLS), Jindal Global Business School (JGBS), Jindal School
of International Affairs (JSIA), Jindal School of Government and Public Policy (JSGP), Jindal
School of Liberal Arts & Humanities (JSLH), Jindal School of Journalism & Communication (JSJC),
Jindal School of Art & Architecture (JSAA), Jindal School of Banking & Finance (JSBF), Jindal
School of Environment & Sustainability (JSES), Jindal School of Psychology & Counselling
(JSPC), Jindal School of Languages & Literature (JSLL), Jindal School of Public Health & Human
Development (JSPH), Jindal Institute of Behavioural Sciences (JIBS), Jindal Institute of
Leadership Development and Executive Education (JILDEE), International Institute for Higher
Education Research and Capacity Building (IIHEd), Jindal India Institute (JII), and the Jindal Centre
for Digital Sciences (JCDS).

JGU has been granted "Autonomy" by the UGC and the Ministry of Human Resource
Development, Government of India, for having received the then-highest "A" Grade from NAAC.
JGU has been ranked India's Number 1 Private University for three years running by QS World
University Rankings 2023, and recognized among the Top 150 universities globally under 50
years old by QS Young University Rankings. Jindal Global Law School has been ranked Number 1
in India for five years in a row and is the only Indian law school among the Global Top 100 law
schools (QS World University Rankings by Subject 2024). In 2020, JGU was recognized as an
'Institution of Eminence' by the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Leadership: Founding Vice Chancellor is Professor (Dr.) C. Raj Kumar (also Dean, Jindal Global
Law School). Registrar is Prof. (Dr.) Maharaj K. Pandit. Chief Financial Officer: Mr. Arun Kumar
Jain. Director, Human Resources: Ms. Poonam Sapra. Chief Administrative Officer: Ms. Deepa
Sharma. Controller of Examinations and Chief Proctor: Prof. Karan Latayan.

=== JINDAL GLOBAL LAW SCHOOL (JGLS) ===
Established 2009 as India's first global law school, recognized by the Bar Council of India.
Programmes: 3-year LL.B., 5-year B.A. LL.B. (Hons.), 5-year B.B.A. LL.B. (Hons.), 5-year B.Com
LL.B. (Hons.), 3-year B.A. Legal Studies, B.A. Criminology & Criminal Justice, 1-year LL.M. (multiple
specializations incl. Corporate & Financial Law, IP & Technology Law, Alternative Dispute
Resolution, International Trade & Investment Law, Taxation Law, Human Rights, Constitutional &
Administrative Law, Criminal Law & Criminal Justice, International Law, General Legal Studies,
Environmental Law/Energy/Climate Change with WWF-India). Ranked #1 Law School in India by QS
World University Rankings by Subject (Law) for 2020-2024. 16 specialised research centres
(e.g., Centre for Human Rights Studies, Centre for Women's Rights, Centre for Alternative Dispute
Resolution). Academic collaborations with Harvard Law School, Yale Law School, King's College
London, University of Sydney Law School, Monash University.

Eligibility & Admissions:
- 5-year B.A./B.B.A./B.Com LL.B. (Hons.): merit in LSAT-India Entrance Test only.
- 3-year B.A. Legal Studies / Criminology & Criminal Justice: LSAT-India or JSAT-Law.
- 3-year LL.B.: LSAT-India only. Minimum: Bachelor's degree, at least 45% aggregate marks.
- 1-year LL.M.: LSAT-India, JSAT-Law, CLAT PG or CUET PG (70% weightage) + SOP/CV (30%).
- Ph.D.: LL.M. with at least 55% marks or equivalent.
LSAT-India registration fee: Rs. 3,999 (via lsatindia.in). Application via lawadmissions.jgu.edu.in.
Fully refundable security deposit: Rs. 50,000 at admission. Dining halls serve vegetarian food;
non-veg available on payment at other outlets. Hostel accommodation is double/triple/four-sharing
only - no single occupancy. Programme fee and residence/dining/laundry charges may rise up to
10% annually.

=== JINDAL GLOBAL BUSINESS SCHOOL (JGBS) ===
Established 2010. 14 programmes for AY 2024-25 including Integrated B.B.A.(Hons.)+MBA (5-yr,
232 credits, Fee Rs. 3,50,000/yr + Residential Rs. 3,10,000/yr + Application Rs. 2,500), B.B.A.(Hons.)
(180 credits, Fee Rs. 4,00,000/yr), B.B.A.(H) Business Analytics/Family Business/Financial Markets
(Fee Rs. 4,50,000/yr), MBA 2-year (103 credits, Tuition Rs. 5,50,000/yr, requires CAT/MAT/XAT/
CMAT/ATMA 50+ percentile or GRE 295+/GMAT 450+/NMAT 160+), Global MBA Business Analytics
1-year (90 credits, Rs. 11,00,000/yr, needs 2 yrs work experience), plus online programmes: MBA
powered by upGrad (1-yr, Rs. 1,50,000), MBA Business & Law with JGLS (1-yr, Rs. 1,50,000), MBA
Business Analytics via Coursera (1-yr Rs.1,65,000 / 2-yr Rs. 4,87,500), MBA Digital Finance via
upGrad (2-yr, Rs. 3,50,000). Full-time Ph.D.: only 2 merit-based fellowship seats/year.
160+ full-time faculty, 415+ cross-listed electives from 11 other schools, 120+ international partner
institutions across 29 countries (e.g., Bond University, Western Sydney University, Pace
University, University of Texas at Dallas). Career support via Office of Career Services (OCS) +
school-level OCPD.

=== JINDAL SCHOOL OF INTERNATIONAL AFFAIRS (JSIA) ===
Commenced 2011 with M.A. Diplomacy, Law & Business (first of its kind in Asia). Also offers B.A.
(Hons.) Global Affairs, B.A. (Hons.) Political Science, B.A. (Hons.) Diplomacy and Foreign Policy,
Ph.D. (since 2016), and online M.A. International Relations, Security & Strategy (with Coursera).
Global Library holds approximately 79,000 volumes. Fee for B.A. programmes: Rs. 3,50,000/yr +
Residential Rs. 3,10,000/yr + Application Rs. 2,500; Programme is 4 years, admission via personal
statement + Class 10/12 marks + faculty interview + JSAT score of 55%+ (or LSAT-India/SAT/ACT).
M.A. (DLB): Rs. 2,50,000/yr, 2-year programme, entrance exam + interview. Ph.D.: two full-time
fellowships worth Rs. 35,000, completed within 3 years (full-time) or 4 years (part-time). Foreign
languages offered: Arabic, French, Mandarin, Spanish, German.

=== JINDAL SCHOOL OF GOVERNMENT & PUBLIC POLICY (JSGP) ===
Offers B.A./B.Sc. (Hons.) Economics (4-yr, 180 credits total for the Hons. track), M.A. Public Policy
(2-yr, 88 credits, includes capstone/dissertation option, entrance via Public Policy Entrance Test +
interview), M.A. Economics (2-yr, 80 credits, entrance test + interview), and Ph.D. (Master's degree
with 55%+ marks). Research centres include the Policy Research Lab (PRL) and Centre for Legal
Empowerment of the Poor.

=== JINDAL SCHOOL OF LIBERAL ARTS & HUMANITIES (JSLH) ===
4-year B.A. (Hons.) Liberal Arts & Humanities (194 credits minimum; majors from Semester 4
onward in Economics, International Business, Literary Studies, Sociology & Anthropology,
Political Science, Philosophy, Psychology, Environmental Studies, History, Self-Designed, plus
new majors Digital Humanities/Marketing Arts & Culture/Comparative Religion from 2024).
2+2 dual degree pathway with Rollins College (USA) and Deakin University (Australia). Also offers
Jindal Fellowship Programme (1-yr PG Diploma in Research & Innovation, Rs. 6,00,000/yr), B.F.A.
(Hons.) (4-yr, 196 credits), and B.A. (Hons.) Sociology (4-yr). At least 4 internships required for
the B.A. (Hons.) LAH programme.

=== JINDAL SCHOOL OF JOURNALISM & COMMUNICATION (JSJC) ===
Three 4-year undergraduate programmes: B.A. (Hons.) Journalism and Media Studies, B.A. Film
and New Media, B.A. (Hons.) Corporate Communication and Public Affairs. Admission via personal
statement + Class X/XII marks + faculty interview + JSAT score of 55%+ (or LSAT-India/SAT/ACT).

=== JINDAL SCHOOL OF ART & ARCHITECTURE (JSAA) ===
Launched 2018. Programmes: Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch, 5-yr, entrance via NATA 50%+,
Fee Rs. 4 Lakh/yr + Hostel Rs. 3.10 Lakh/yr); Bachelor of Design (B.Des, entrance via JCAT 50%+
in each section or UCEED, Fee Rs. 3 Lakh/yr, pathways: Interior Environments, Immersive Digital
Environments); BA (Hons.) Built Environment Studies - Jindal Adelaide Architecture Pathway
(3 years at JSAA + transfer to University of Adelaide year 4, Fee at JSAA Rs. 4.5 Lakh/yr, at
Adelaide 42,000 AUD/yr) or Jindal Pathway (4-yr residency at JGU, Fee Rs. 4.5 Lakh/yr). 18 minor
programme areas available (e.g., Anthropology, Legal Studies, Public Policy, Psychology, Public
Health, Community & Urban Development).

=== JINDAL SCHOOL OF BANKING & FINANCE (JSBF) ===
Started 2018 with B.Com. (Hons.). Now also offers B.A. (Hons.) Finance & Entrepreneurship
(4-yr, fully residential), Global B.Com. (transfer to Deakin University, Australia - pathway to
Bachelor of Business + MBA International from Deakin), B.Com. (Hons.) Capital Markets (with
NSE Academy Ltd., supports CFA/CFP/FRM prep), M.Sc. Finance (1-yr), and Ph.D. in Finance.
All UG programmes are 4 years per NEP 2020; PG (M.Sc. Finance) is 1 year.
